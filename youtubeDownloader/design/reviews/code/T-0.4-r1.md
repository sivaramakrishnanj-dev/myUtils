## N1 — Fat-jar size tracking: 5.65 MB exceeds M0 soft target

- **Location:** tester handoff (artifact-shape check)
- **Severity:** Nit
- **Category:** Discussion
- **Spec citation:** Risk R-6 in 07-tasks.md § 5; M0 gate says "Fat jar under 5 MB (just dependencies, no logic)"
- **Reviewer comment:**
  The tester reports the shaded jar at 5.65 MB, which exceeds the M0 gate's 5 MB soft target. R-6 explicitly anticipates this due to OkHttp 4.x's transitive kotlin-stdlib dependency and prescribes tracking size at every milestone with a potential move to OkHttp 5.x if the 10 MB hard cap is threatened. No action needed now — this is informational for milestone-gate awareness.
- **Suggested fix:**
  None required. Consider adding a comment in the M0 gate checklist noting the actual size for future tracking.
