- **Round-2 status:** Fixed. The implementer added the exact overflow guard suggested:
  ```java
  long required = (expectedBytes > Long.MAX_VALUE / FREE_SPACE_MULTIPLIER)
          ? Long.MAX_VALUE
  ```

  At `OutputWriter.java:111–113`. When `expectedBytes > Long.MAX_VALUE / 2`, the result saturates to `Long.MAX_VALUE` instead of wrapping to a negative value.
